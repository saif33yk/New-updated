Frequently Asked Questions

To join our server, you need to have FiveM installed and join our Discord server. After that, you'll need to complete the whitelist application process.

Our server rules can be found in the #rules channel on our Discord server. They include guidelines for roleplay, behavior, and server etiquette.

You can report players through our Discord server using the #report channel or by submitting a ticket through our support system.

You need a valid copy of GTA V, the latest version of FiveM, and a Discord account. A microphone is recommended for voice chat.
